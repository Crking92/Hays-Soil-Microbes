Simple explanation

Soil is not just dirt. It is habitat. The minerals, clay, limestone, roots, water, air, and organic matter in soil shape which plants grow well and what kinds of soil life can thrive.

This dashboard can help you

  • Understand broad soil differences
  • Choose better native plants for a site
  • Explain soil health benefits
  • See likely microbial functions
  • Ask better questions before amending soil

This dashboard cannot yet

  • Test your exact yard
  • Tell you exactly what fertilizer to apply
  • Prove a microbe is present at one property
  • Replace a lab soil test
  • Measure active nutrient cycling rates
