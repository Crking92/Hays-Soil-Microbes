What this dashboard is

This dashboard combines Kyle + ETJ soil map data, lab-supported soil context, and regional NEON/NMDC microbial evidence. It helps explain how local soil conditions may support nutrient cycling, water holding, native plant restoration, carbon storage, and microbial functional potential.

Important: regional microbial data are not local proof for every yard, park, or restoration site. Local confirmation requires local soil sampling or lab testing.
