Read this before reading the tables

1. Soil is the local habitat layer Soil maps and lab pedons describe the physical and chemical setting: texture, pH, clay, carbonate, drainage, depth, and related clues.
2. Microbial data are regional analog evidence NEON/NMDC records show what was observed in similar regional datasets, not proof for every point in Hays County.
3. Function data mean potential KO, EC, and product-name hits suggest genetic potential, not measured activity.
4. Use evidence grades Do not make a local confirmation claim unless the data layer is actually local.
