What, why, and how this dashboard works

What This dashboard layers mapped soil data, lab-supported soil properties, regional observed microbes, and processed gene/function annotations.
Why Soil conditions shape water, nutrients, roots, microbial habitat, restoration potential, and native plant fit.
How Local soil data provide the habitat frame. Regional NEON/NMDC data provide microbial analog evidence. Evidence labels prevent overclaiming.
How to read it Start with soil context, then check regional taxa/functions, then confirm the evidence grade before making a claim.
Beginner translation of the science

Soil maps tell us what kind of habitat is likely present. Microbial datasets tell us what microbes and genes were observed in similar regional soils. Combining them helps explain likely soil processes, but it does not prove the same microbes or processes are present at every point in Kyle.

Overview: what the summaries mean

The function cards below are broad categories created from processed gene/product annotations. They are useful for seeing major potential soil functions, but they are not direct measurements of activity.

Lab pedon support

Lab pedons provide measured soil context such as pH, chemistry, texture, and related properties where available.

Soils: the local habitat frame

Soil data are the local foundation. They help explain what kind of microbial habitat and restoration setting is likely present.

Observed regional taxa

These are observed in processed regional analog outputs. They help identify microbial groups known from comparable datasets, but they are not local Hays/Kyle confirmations.

Functional potential

KO/EC/product-name hits indicate genes or annotations associated with potential functions. They do not prove those functions were active at sampling time.

Top KEGG Orthology IDs

KO IDs are gene/function categories used for microbial functional annotation.

Top Enzyme Commission IDs

EC IDs describe enzyme types. They are useful for broad biochemical potential.

Top product names

Product names are readable gene annotations. Many will be broad or hypothetical.

Evidence grades

Always check this section before making a claim. It separates local confirmation, regional observation, soil-habitat inference, and general reference interpretation.
