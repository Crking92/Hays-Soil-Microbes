How to use this research view

What you are looking at

This page is a layered evidence dashboard. It starts with mapped soil habitat, then adds lab pedon support, observed regional microbial taxa, and functional-potential annotations from processed metagenome outputs.

  1. Start with soils. Search for a soil, component, texture, habitat class, or management keyword.
  2. Check observed taxa. These are regional analog observations, not local Kyle confirmations.
  3. Check functions. KO/EC/product hits show functional potential, not measured activity.
  4. Use the evidence tab. It explains how confident each kind of claim should be.
