The main idea

Healthy soil works like a living system. Plants feed soil life through roots and leaf litter. Soil life helps recycle nutrients, build structure, and support resilient plant communities.

Important limit

This dashboard does not test your exact yard. It combines local soil maps with regional microbial evidence to explain what the soil is likely able to support.

How to use this page

Why soil microbes matter in a garden or restoration site

You do not need to memorize microbe names. Focus on the jobs they help with.
