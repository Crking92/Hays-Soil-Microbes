Start with the big idea

Think of soil as habitat

Soil is where roots, water, air, minerals, organic matter, bacteria, fungi, and tiny soil animals interact. A good garden or restoration plan works with that habitat instead of fighting it.

Do not start with microbe names

Beginners do not need to memorize microbes. Start with the jobs: recycle nutrients, build structure, help roots, buffer drought, and process organic matter.

The soil food web in plain language

The soil food web is the living network belowground. Plants feed microbes. Microbes recycle organic matter. Tiny predators release nutrients. Roots and fungi help build structure.

Why are there numbers here?

The numbers are counts of regional gene/function signals from processed microbial data. A bigger number usually means that signal appeared more often in this dataset. It does not mean the function is active in your exact yard.
