How to read the result

Soil story A short, plain-language description of the mapped soil.
Root meaning What clay, limestone, drainage, depth, and organic matter may mean for plants.
Microbe jobs Soil-life functions grouped by job, not gene code.
Evidence label A reminder that this is a habitat prediction, not a direct microbe test.

After you find your soil

Beginner next step Open My Soil Coach and match the map result to what you see in the yard.
Best confirmation Do one backyard test: water soak-in, ribbon texture, or shovel/root-zone check.
Plant choice Match plants to soil habitat before adding amendments or fertilizer.
Evidence caution The map predicts habitat; exact microbes require local sampling.
