How this works

The map checks which loaded Kyle/ETJ soil polygon contains your selected point. It then translates the soil record into plain-language clues for gardening, native plants, restoration, and likely soil-life benefits.

Good for

  • Understanding likely soil setting
  • Planting and restoration conversations
  • Seeing drainage, clay, limestone, or shallow-soil clues
  • Explaining likely microbial habitat benefits

Not a substitute for

  • A property-level soil test
  • Local microbial sequencing
  • Fertilizer prescriptions
  • Engineering or regulatory determinations
